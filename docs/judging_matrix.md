# Judging matrix → evidence in project
| Criterion (weight) | Evidence | Honest caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Bio-economy relevance & impact (25%) | NTFP batches (tamarind/honey), handling screening, net-return selling decisions, traceability with sustainability-doc status | Impact not measured; pilot needed (see below) |
| Innovation (20%) | Real price seasonality + forecast linked to each batch's storage screening in a hold-or-sell decision, with net-return and traceability in one workflow and explicit data-status labels | Uniqueness not researched; do not claim "first" |
| Feasibility (20%) | Working app, 40 passing tests, real 204k-row dataset, trained model with chronological test and model card, transparent formulas | Gain modest; tamarind-only gain not statistically proven; risk rules unvalidated |
| Scalability / viability (20%) | Local-first, free stack; users: producer groups, cooperatives, small traders; pathways below | No customers, partners or revenue exist |
| Presentation (15%) | Scripted 5-min demo, clean UI, backup plan | Visuals untested on projector |
**Pilot design:** 1–2 producer groups; record batches 4–8 weeks; compare loss rates with a baseline period and recorded vs realised prices; track adoption; expert review of thresholds.
**Business pathway (hypothesis):** subscription/licence for cooperatives or NGOs, buyer-side traceability service; costs = support, training, hosting. Unvalidated.
